ABOUT

Builded replaces the usual patchwork of agencies, freelancers, and late hand-offs with one seamless pipeline. Inside our studio, brand strategists, interface designers, artists, and front-end engineers sit side-by-side, translating product vision into a unified stack of names, marks, type systems, motion specs, design tokens, coded components, and launch-ready prototypes.

We plug in a full-stack design squad—strategists, UI/UX architects, and engineers—directly into your workflow.
